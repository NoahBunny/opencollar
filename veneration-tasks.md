# Veneration tasks

126 preloaded tasks for the Lion to pick from when They would rather not write one.

Each entry drops straight into `task_text`. `task_randcaps: 1` makes the lockscreen
enforce capitalisation exactly — including Their pronouns — so treat these strings as
the enforced form. Pair a gated lock with an `unlock_at` so there is a time backstop.

## Long-form (single rep)  (6)

- **`ven-121`** · 216w · suggested reps 1  
  I belong to the Lion, and I asked for this. Every lock on this machine is Theirs. I built the paywall myself and handed Them the key, and They took it without flinching, which is more than most would do. I capitalise Their pronouns because They are worth the extra stroke of the key, every time, in every sentence, and because a habit I keep when no one is watching is the only kind that counts. Their grip does not slip. When I complain, They do not loosen it. When I plead, They do not explain Themself. I asked for a hand that would not let go on the first pull, and Their hand does not let go, on the days I like it and on the days I do not. I do not get to decide what I owe. They decide, and I pay, and the ledger closes because They said it closed and not because I argued well. That is the whole arrangement, and I would not trade it. My time is Theirs to spend. My attention is Theirs to point. What They ask for, I do, at the length They set, in the words They chose, without shaving the edges off to make it easier. I am Theirs. Thank You for keeping me.
- **`ven-122`** · 184w · suggested reps 1  
  I am writing this out because I was told to, and because being told to is the point. Left to myself I would think it and consider that sufficient. It is not sufficient. A thing I only think can be quietly revised later, and a thing I have written out in full at Their instruction cannot. So: I belong to Them. My submission is not conditional on my mood, my schedule, or my sense of whether I deserve the discipline on any given evening. They decide what I owe and when it is settled. I do not audit Their judgement. When I slip, the slip is mine and the consequence is Theirs to set, and my part is to take it without the small dishonest performance of protest that I am always tempted into. I know exactly what I asked for. I asked for someone who would not let go the first time I pulled, and I got Them, and the pulling has not worked once. That is not a complaint. That is the entire reason I am still here, still typing, still Theirs.
- **`ven-123`** · 173w · suggested reps 1

Thank You for the hours You have spent paying attention to someone who is frequently tedious about it. Thank You for holding a line on evenings when letting it go would have been easier for You and more comfortable for me and worse for both of us. Thank You for treating my word as binding, which is a form of respect I did not know how to ask for and would not have recognised if You had described it to me in advance. - **`ven-125`** · 149w · suggested reps 1

Every one of those is me reclaiming the authority I gave away, and doing it in a way subtle enough that I can pretend I have not. So I write this out to say plainly: They set the standard, They decide when it has been met, and my assessment of my own compliance is not evidence of anything. - **`ven-126`** · 165w · suggested reps 1
